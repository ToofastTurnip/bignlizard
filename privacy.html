Privacy Policy

Short version: we're not much for record-keeping. This site doesn't ask you to log in, doesn't track you around, and has nothing worth selling. Here's the full account anyway.

What We Collect

Big N Lizard doesn't collect personal information through this website. There's no account system, no shopping cart, no newsletter sign-up, no ad network, and no tracking scripts embedded in these pages. Even our fonts are hosted here rather than fetched from Google Fonts, so no third party learns you stopped by.

Visit Counts, via Cloudflare

This site is served through Cloudflare, our hosting and CDN provider. As part of ordinary infrastructure, Cloudflare keeps basic, aggregate visit counts: roughly how many people stopped by and when. We look at this to see whether anyone's reading the tavern notices, not to identify individual visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle it.

Google Search Console

We also use Google Search Console, a free tool that shows us how bignlizard.com turns up in Google Search: which searches led people here, whether Google can crawl the site properly, and the like. It's run by Google under Google's own privacy policy, and it only ever hands us aggregate search performance data, not anything tied to you personally.

Cookies

We don't set any cookies ourselves. If your browser shows one from this domain, it's coming from Cloudflare's infrastructure for basic security and performance, not from us tracking you across the site or the web.

Leaving the Tavern

A few links on this site lead elsewhere, most notably the playable Pantheon Loreholders demo hosted on Hugging Face. Once you click through, you're on their turf: their own privacy policy and terms apply, not ours. We don't send them anything about you, and we don't see anything about what you do once you're there.

If You Write to Us

If you email us, say at mitch@bignlizard.com, we'll see your email address and whatever you tell us. We use it only to write back, and we won't sell it, rent it, or hand it off to anyone else.

Changes to This Notice

If this policy changes, we'll update the date below. Given how little we collect, we don't expect much to move.

Contact

Questions about any of this? Email mitch@bignlizard.com.
